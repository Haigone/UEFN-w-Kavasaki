# NARRATIVE MASTER SCRIPT — THE MOTEL: ROOM 13

**Status:** Canonical beat script assembled from existing specifications; not a final dialogue script. All in-game content is English. New authored lines are PROPOSAL.

## Premise
In 1986, four friends are stranded by a storm near the officially closed Raven Motel. Its neon sign illuminates at 02:13. Reception contains a key for a thirteenth room although the public motel has twelve rooms. The phone welcomes the group as if expected. Records and impossible spatial evidence reveal that guests have disappeared into a repeating night. The owner and THE GUEST are the same person or manifestations of the same identity; ROOM 13 confirms their visual identity through matching portrait, records and creature appearance.

## Tone and delivery
Psychological dread; restrained dialogue; environmental storytelling; no exposition dump. Mandatory information must be available visually or through subtitles. The playable friends remain lightly characterized. Preserve ambiguity before ROOM 13. Keep the 02:13 motif consistent.

## ACT I — THE ARRIVAL (0–8 min)
Exterior storm, broken car and neon sign. Brief tall silhouette on upper balcony; no forced chase. Players enter reception and see twelve ordinary room keys. ROOM 13 key is present despite no public room 13. Phone rings and plays the approved line: “Welcome. We've been expecting you.” Objective: investigate front desk and find a way into the owner's office.

## ACT II — THE REGISTER (8–17 min)
Inspect register and solve the three-part date/number code. Open drawer and obtain Office Key. Enter owner's office; inspect records, calendar and photographs. Solve the office photo clue to gain upper-floor access. Explore rooms 01–06: 01 impossible CRT corridor feed; 02 suitcase and flashlight battery; 03 cracked mirror reflects THE GUEST; 04 telephone clue recording; 05 footsteps above ceiling; 06 missing guest photographs. Objective handoff: restore power. Exact register entries/code and office photo solution are not in the source package.

## ACT III — THE POWER (17–27 min)
Find storage/electrical room. Locate Fuse A/B/C in distinct spaces and install into clearly labelled slots. Restore power; lights and selected systems change. Activation sound draws THE GUEST to investigate. Trigger first full pursuit with clear cues, reachable route and regroup opportunity. Do not add a fuse sequence unless a clue establishes it.

## ACT IV — THE SECOND FLOOR (27–36 min)
Explore rooms 07–12 and balcony. ROOM 09 diary is primary history evidence. ROOM 11 contains child's room, toys and unsettling drawing. ROOM 12 contains sealed wall/concealed panel. Open secret passage and allow team regrouping. Reachability must be verified in UEFN.

## ACT V — ROOM 13 (36–43 min)
Enter impossible ROOM 13 through passage. Inspect bed, wardrobe, CRT, phone, photographs, owner portrait and clock frozen at 02:13. Arrange four photographs chronologically. Hidden compartment opens; matching portrait, records and creature appearance establish owner/GUEST identity. Obtain Service Key or unlock route to it. Set reveal flag only after reveal plays. Full truth evidence checklist remains undefined.

## ACT VI — NO VACANCY (43–50 min)
Reach service route. Recover Car Keys if not already team-owned. Use Service Key at service mechanism; activate gate/generator as final layout requires. Trigger final pursuit with readable wayfinding. Evaluate explicit branch choice, lock one ending and play unique sequence/title card.

## Endings
### ESCAPE
Players reach car and escape through gate during chase. Approved radio: “Thank you for staying at the Raven Motel. We hope to see you again soon.” Silhouette appears in rear seat or rear-view reflection; cut to black.

### THE TRUTH
Confirm owner identity as THE GUEST, then use truth route to confront/expose the source. A distinct cinematic and title card are required. Exact confrontation action, cost, dialogue and consequence are absent and require approval; do not fabricate canon.

### THE NEW GUEST
Explicitly accept invitation or enter separately authored cycle branch. Motel resets; one or more players become part of next story; neon sign lights at 02:13. In co-op use team confirmation/shared outcome, never random selection without consent. Ordinary capture or timer expiry must not trigger it.
